Carbon

Every reader gets a copy that looks identical and isn't. If a version leaks, the text says who held it.

The marks are four zero-width Unicode characters carrying a base-4 payload, repeated every 40 words so a partial copy still identifies itself. They survive copy, paste and most re-typing of the file. They do not survive an AI rewrite, a screenshot, or a deliberate strip — nothing does.

This is forensic, not preventive. It answers “who leaked it,” not “can they copy it.” Pair it with a dated registration and an NDA, which is what actually wins the argument.
